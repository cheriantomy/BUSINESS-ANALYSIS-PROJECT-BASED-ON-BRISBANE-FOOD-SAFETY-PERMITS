BUSINESS ANALYSIS PROJECT BASED ON BRISBANE FOOD SAFETY PERMITS

# 1)	Business Understanding Document

Business Problem: Based on the data set, the data consists of the food safety permits for different businesses across Brisbane, it consists of the names of the businesses, eat safe ratings, permit name, addresses, and the suburb on which the business exists. From the analysis, these are the problem statements,


1.1)	 Business name and Suburb: goal is to identify the suburb where most businesses tend to happen (could be within different parts of Brisbane and outside of Brisbane)

1.2)	Business name and address: Goal is to identify the businesses with the licensing and registration when compared to other businesses.

1.3)	 Business name and eat safe permit: Could be to identify those businesses who’s safe eat permits are below 5.

1.4)	Permit name and eat safe rating: goal is to identify those business categories or permit names where eat safe rating is more efficient and less efficient and to figure out the reasons for it.

1.5)	Business name and business phone number: goal is to identify that business with or without the phone numbers.

1.6)	Permit name and suburb: Goal is to identify those businesses whose food category or permit name happens most or less based on the Suburbs.


# 2)	Business objectives:

ID	Business objective	Analysis

BO1	Improve the visibility of Businesses	Improve the visibility based on business, suburbs, eat safe rating and permit name

BO2	Improve the visibility of eat safe ratings.	Try to find out the safe ratings for business based on food categories or permit name and the suburbs prioritizing based on the ratings.

BO3	Improve the data quality	Find out the repeating values, null values and missing values from the dataset. (for eg..Business phone number)

BO4	businesses based on the addresses. (not important as less chance to figure out)	Could be used to identify the addresses with licensing and registration of businesses as all businesses have licenses and registrations. :;

BO6	Improve management reporting. 	Could be figured out with the help of KPI’S and dashboards.
